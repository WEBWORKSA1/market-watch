FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Market.Watch free?

Yes. Every dashboard, calculator and guide is free. The site is funded by advertising, sponsorships and voluntary reader support.

Is the market data real-time?

Charts and quotes are supplied by TradingView. Many crypto and forex feeds are real-time; some exchange data is delayed by 15 minutes or more depending on the exchange’s licensing.

Do you give investment advice?

No. Market.Watch is an educational and informational resource. We do not know your circumstances, so nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell anything. Read our disclaimer.

Do you store my watchlist or calculator inputs?

No. Your watchlist is saved only in your own browser (local storage), and calculators run entirely on your device.

Are you affiliated with MarketWatch?

No. Market.Watch is a fully independent website with no connection to MarketWatch or Dow Jones. See our trademark disclosure.
