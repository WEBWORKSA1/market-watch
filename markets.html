How to use this dashboard

Start broad, then narrow. Glance at the ticker tape for the overall mood: are the major indices, Bitcoin and the US dollar moving together or apart? Then open the heatmap for your asset class to see whether a move is broad (most blocks one colour) or concentrated in a few large names.

Use the advanced chart for context. Switch time frames with the range buttons at the bottom. A 2% drop looks dramatic on a one-day chart and trivial on a five-year chart. Add indicators such as the 50- and 200-day moving averages from the chart toolbar to see the longer trend. Our guide on reading stock charts walks through each tool.

Check the calendar before reacting. Many big moves happen around scheduled data releases. The economic calendar shows what is coming and how markets usually respond.

Symbol format: type EXCHANGE:TICKER for exact matches, for example NASDAQ:MSFT, NYSE:JPM, TSX:SHOP, NSE:RELIANCE, BINANCE:ETHUSDT or FX:GBPUSD.
