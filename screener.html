Five screens worth trying

Quality at a fair price

P/E below the market average, positive earnings growth and a return on equity above 15%. A classic starting point for long-term investors.

Dividend growers

Yield between 2% and 5% with a payout ratio under 60%. Very high yields often signal risk rather than value.

Momentum leaders

Price above the 50- and 200-day moving averages and a strong six-month performance. Pair with a stop-loss plan.

Oversold large caps

Market cap above $10B with RSI under 30. Useful for spotting sell-offs, but oversold can stay oversold.

Unusual volume

Relative volume above 2×. Heavy trading often accompanies news; check the cause before acting.

Screening is the start of research, not the end

A screener narrows thousands of possibilities to a short list. It cannot tell you why a stock is cheap or why it is surging. Treat every result as a question: read the latest earnings report, look at the long-term chart, and understand what the business sells and who it competes with.
