Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Market.Watch itself sets no tracking cookies; it uses your browser’s local storage for a few preferences. Our partners may set cookies as described below.

Name / providerTypePurpose
mw-theme, mw-watchlist, mw-consent, mw-pop, mw-lead (Market.Watch)Local storage, essential/preferenceRemember your theme, watchlist, cookie choice and whether you have seen our newsletter prompt. Never leaves your device.
Google AdSense (Google and partners)AdvertisingServe and measure ads, limit repetition, and (with consent where required) personalise ads.
TradingViewFunctionalDeliver embedded charts and market data widgets.
YouTube (privacy-enhanced)FunctionalPlay embedded videos, only after you click play.

Managing cookies

  • Use the cookie banner to choose “Essential only” or “Accept all”. To change your choice, clear this site’s storage in your browser and reload.
  • Opt out of personalised Google ads at Google Ads Settings.
  • Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings. Blocking some cookies may affect embedded charts and videos.

See our Privacy Policy for more information.
