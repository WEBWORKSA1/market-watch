How to get the most from these calculators

Use conservative assumptions. Long-run stock market returns have historically averaged high single digits before inflation, but no return is guaranteed and sequences of bad years happen. Run each scenario at a pessimistic, base and optimistic rate.

Risk first, reward second. Professional traders decide how much they are willing to lose before they think about upside. The position size and risk/reward calculators enforce that discipline. Our guide on risk management explains the maths.

Remember inflation and fees. A 7% nominal return with 3% inflation is roughly 4% in real purchasing power. A 1% annual fee can consume a large share of long-term gains. See compound interest and dollar-cost averaging.

Calculators are educational estimates, not financial advice, and do not account for taxes or your personal situation.
