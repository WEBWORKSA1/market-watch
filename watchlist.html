Building a useful watchlist

A good watchlist is short. Ten to twenty names you understand beats a hundred you skim. Mix in a benchmark (such as SPY) so you can tell whether a holding is moving on its own news or just with the market.

Group ideas by purpose: long-term holdings, companies you would buy at a lower price, and macro signals like the US 10-year yield, oil and the dollar.
