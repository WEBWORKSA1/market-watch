About Market.Watch

We believe everyone deserves professional-grade market tools and honest, jargon-free explanations, without paywalls.

Our mission

Financial markets shape mortgages, pensions, prices at the grocery store and the value of every saver’s nest egg. Yet the best market data and analysis often sit behind expensive subscriptions, and much free content is either too shallow or designed to sell you something. Market.Watch exists to close that gap: live dashboards that rival paid platforms, calculators that answer real questions, and guides that explain the why behind the numbers.

What we offer

  • Live markets: indices, stocks, ETFs, crypto, forex, bonds and commodities with interactive charts and heatmaps.
  • Research tools: a multi-market screener, an economic calendar and a private watchlist.
  • Calculators: eleven tools for compounding, risk, valuation, income and planning.
  • Education: in-depth guides, a 120+ term glossary and a curated video library.
  • Community: free skill-based contests with real prizes.

Editorial standards

Our guides are written to be accurate, balanced and useful. We explain concepts rather than tip stocks, show both the benefits and risks of every strategy, and update articles when facts change. We correct errors promptly; if you spot one, please tell us. Sponsored content is always labelled and never influences our editorial guides.

Where our data comes from

Charts, quotes, heatmaps, screeners, news headlines and the economic calendar are provided by TradingView under its free widget programme. Depending on the exchange, data may be real-time or delayed. Videos are embedded from YouTube and belong to their creators.

How we are funded

Market.Watch is free to use. We earn revenue from display advertising (Google AdSense), clearly labelled sponsorships and voluntary reader support. Advertisers have no say over our educational content.

Independence

Market.Watch is an independent website. We are not affiliated with MarketWatch, Dow Jones & Company, any broker, exchange or financial institution. Read our full disclaimer and trademark notice.
