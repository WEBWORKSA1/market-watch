How to learn from finance videos without getting misled

Video is a brilliant teacher and a risky advisor. Favour creators who explain concepts over those promising returns. Be wary of thumbnails with huge percentage gains, “secret” strategies, or any pitch for a paid group that guarantees profits. Check whether the presenter discloses positions and sponsors.

After each video, test yourself: can you explain the idea in one sentence? Then apply it with a calculator, for example run the compound growth calculator after a compounding lesson, or the position size calculator after a risk lesson.
